JAMZ WORLD - HOME SCREEN APP

Put everything in this folder on any website (it must be https). Free option, GitHub Pages:
 1. Make a free account at github.com and create a new public repository, e.g. "jamzworld".
 2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files" and drag in every file from this folder. Commit.
 3. Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder: / (root). Save.
 4. After a minute your game is at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/jamzworld/
On the iPhone: open that link in Safari > Share button > "Add to Home Screen".
It opens full screen in landscape, works offline, and keeps its saves.
To update: upload the new index.html over the old one.
